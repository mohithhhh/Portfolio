# Temporal Belief Dynamics in BERT

**Research project** · Jun 2025 – Jul 2025

## What it is

A study of how BERT's internal "beliefs" change while it is being fine-tuned.

- Mapped the evolution of BERT's internal "beliefs" across **10 fine-tuning epochs**, using graph-theoretic networks and 2D embeddings to uncover and visualize hidden representation drift.
- Shared these findings in a research paper that was accepted for publication at the **7th International Conference on Communication and Intelligent Systems 2025**.

## Publication

M.D.K. et al., *"Temporal Belief Dynamics in BERT: A Graph-Theoretic Approach to Interpretable Representation Drift,"* published at the 7th International Conference on Communication and Intelligent Systems (ICCIS 2025), BITS Pilani, Goa, *Springer LNCS*.

## Where it came from

This work started during my research internship at RAPID Research Lab (May – Jun 2025).
