# DawaiSathi

**Multilingual AI medicine explainer** · Jul 2026 · [dawaisathi.vercel.app](https://dawaisathi.vercel.app/)

## What it does

- Launched an MVP of a consumer-facing pharma service (**10+ users**): snap a strip or type a medicine name to get plain-language usage, side effects and warnings in **11 Indian languages** via Gemini, streamed over SSE.

## Product

- Developed the full-stack product with **Next.js 14, Supabase (PostgreSQL, Google OAuth) and Razorpay** payments with tiered pricing; added follow-up medicine chat, explanation history, and Vercel deployment.

**Stack:** Gemini · Next.js 14 · Supabase · PostgreSQL · Google OAuth · Razorpay · SSE · Vercel
