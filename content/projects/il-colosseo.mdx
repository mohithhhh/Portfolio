# Il Colosseo

**Real-time multi-agent AI debate arena** · Aug 2026 · [ilcolosseo.vercel.app](https://ilcolosseo.vercel.app/)

## What it does

- Built a real-time **multi-agent AI debate platform** with **3 agents** (two Gemini debaters arguing opposing sides plus an AI judge) over three rounds, grounded in **live web research (Tavily)** and streamed via **FastAPI and SSE**.

## Quality layer

- Engineered a quality layer (topic classifier, source-integrity gate, speech evaluator) and a deep-research pipeline for grounded, citable arguments.
- Added **multilingual debates in 10 Indian languages** with voice output; deployed the frontend on Vercel.

**Stack:** Gemini · Tavily · FastAPI · SSE · Vercel
