# Publication: ICCIS 2025

M.D.K. et al., *"Temporal Belief Dynamics in BERT: A Graph-Theoretic Approach to Interpretable Representation Drift,"* published at the 7th International Conference on Communication and Intelligent Systems (ICCIS 2025), BITS Pilani, Goa, *Springer LNCS*.

The paper maps the evolution of BERT's internal "beliefs" across 10 fine-tuning epochs using graph-theoretic networks and 2D embeddings.
